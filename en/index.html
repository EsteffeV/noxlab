01 — Services

You have a question. We place sensors to answer it.

  • “When do they switch off?”

    Fatigue & vigilance

    Drowsiness and drops in attention at the wheel or at work, tracked second by second (EEG, ECG, EOG, respiration).

  • “Comfortable or tiring?”

    Comfort & posture

    Seats, cabins, workstations: posture through motion capture, physiological load and self-reported feeling.

  • “Which do they really prefer?”

    Sound & ambience

    Sounds, scents and ambiences compared on the body's response, not only on what people say.

  • “What does our data say?”

    Data analysis

    Already have recordings? We clean them, analyse them and tell you what they show.
