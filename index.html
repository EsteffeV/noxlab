01 — Services

You have a question about people. We answer it with evidence.

  • “What does the science say about what we claim?”

    Scientific dossier & pitch

    The state of research on your question, an honest check of your claims, the sensors and protocol to prove them, and a ready-to-use argument for investors, grant applications or clients. No equipment, no participants needed.

  • “Does it work with real users?”

    Pilot study

    A small study on your own scenario: protocol, data collection with your equipment or ours, analysis set against published research, and clear next steps.

  • “Does our device measure what it claims?”

    Sensor & system validation

    Your device or algorithm compared with reference signals: agreement, error, and the conditions where it fails. An independent report you can show — we sell no sensor ourselves.

  • “What do our recordings show?”

    Data analysis

    A quality check first, then processing and statistics with documented Python scripts you keep, and results explained in plain language.

Not sure where to start?

After a short call, we send you a free 2–3 page scoping note: your question restated, what research says, what we would measure, and a fixed price. Protocol, ethics and GDPR documents are included whenever a study needs them.

03 — Approach

The full chain, in one small team.

  1. → 1

    Literature

    What research already says about your question, with sources.

  2. → 2

    Protocol

    Conditions, participants and measures — plus consent, GDPR and ethics checks.

  3. → 3

    Measurement & analysis

    In the lab, in a simulator or on site. Python scripts, documented and yours to keep.

  4. → 4

    Recommendation

    Results set against published research, with their limits, and what to do next.

Scientific discipline

Every claim anchored in published research. Our own results are reported with the conditions under which they hold.

Regulatory awareness

We flag early what the EU AI Act and medical device rules may mean for a product that measures people. Flags, not legal advice.

Independence

We sell no sensor and no software, so our assessment of your system is credible.

What we are not: a neuromarketing agency, a medical diagnosis service, or a seller of sensors or camera systems.
